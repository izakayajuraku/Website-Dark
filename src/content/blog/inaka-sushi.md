Inaka sushi is countryside-style sushi — built around seasonal vegetables rather than raw fish. "Inaka" means countryside, and the style comes out of family-kitchen traditions from rural Japan.

## What Is Inaka Sushi?

It's a vegetable-forward approach that draws on whatever's in season: pickled vegetables, simmered roots, foraged mountain plants, or seasonal mushrooms. We rotate our vegetable nigiri to match.

## Right Now

We're currently featuring Eringi mushroom (king oyster), seared to bring out its umami and served over hand-formed sushi rice with soy. Past seasonal features have included shiso-marinated cucumber, grilled eggplant with miso glaze, pickled daikon, and roasted kabocha squash.

## Why Try It

It's a vegetarian-friendly way into sushi, it shows off Japanese flavors beyond seafood, and it pairs nicely with sake or a light shochu.
