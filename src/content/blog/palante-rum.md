Pa'Lante Rum is a New York-based craft rum brand, and we like having it behind the bar. We pour both their Blanco and Reposado, including as an add-on to our Yuzu Slushy.

## The Story Behind Pa'Lante Rum

Pa'Lante is New York-made, built around Caribbean rum traditions with its own spin. The name means "forward" in Spanish, which fits the brand's approach — pushing rum in new directions without losing the traditions it comes from. Each batch is distilled with sustainability in mind, using thoughtfully sourced ingredients.

## Pa'Lante Rum Expressions at Izakaya Juraku

### Pa'Lante Blanco Rum

- Bright and fresh, with tropical fruit, vanilla, and citrus notes
- Smooth, with a crisp, clean finish
- Goes well with our Yuzu Slushy — yuzu, lemon, and shiso, ultra-refreshing and citrus-forward

### Pa'Lante Reposado Rum

- Aged several months in oak barrels, picking up warm caramel, spice, and toasted vanilla
- More complex, good for sipping or in spirit-forward cocktails
- Pairs well with smoky, umami-heavy Japanese dishes

## How We Serve Pa'Lante Rum at Izakaya Juraku

We don't have a signature cocktail built around Pa'Lante yet, but you can add it to our Yuzu Slushy for extra depth and tropical character.

Try Pa'Lante at Izakaya Juraku, 121 Ludlow St, New York, NY 10002 — Blanco in the Yuzu Slushy, Reposado on its own if you want something to sip slow.
