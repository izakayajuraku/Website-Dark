Some dishes just nail the combination of texture, heat, and flavor — our Crispy Rice is one of them. It's a Japanese-American classic that we've refined with bold ingredients and careful technique.

## What Is Crispy Rice?

Golden, pan-seared rice squares, crispy on the outside and soft and chewy inside. The whole dish is built around contrast: warm and cool, soft and crisp, spicy and refreshing. We serve two versions.

### Spicy Tuna Crispy Rice
- House-made spicy tuna blended with mayo and chili
- Topped with scallion for brightness
- Creamy, punchy, and savory, with a building heat

### Yellowtail Crispy Rice with Yuzu Wasabi
- Buttery hamachi slices on top
- Finished with a zesty yuzu wasabi drizzle
- Diced jalapeño for crunch and spice

## Why It Works

The crunchy rice base holds up well against silky fish and layered flavor — the creamy heat of the spicy tuna, or the clean citrus kick of the yellowtail.

Pair it with a cold beer or highball to cool things down, or a shochu or sake to match the richness.
