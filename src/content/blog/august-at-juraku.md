July was a good one — a fun popup collaboration and a Tanabata celebration that filled the place with guests in yukata. Thank you to everyone who came through. Here's what's coming up in August.

## A Dangerous New Shot

Say hello to XXX FW, a new shot built around Barrow's Intense Ginger. It's a shot with some serious attitude. It's delicious. It's dangerous.

## The Matcha Sake Bomb, Now With a Chant

The Matcha Sake Bomb just got an upgrade — including its own chant: "Matcha, Moeru, Kimi to, Inochi no BOOOOOOMB!" Here's how it works: hold a beer in one hand and a sake shot above it, chant together, then drop the shot into the beer and drink.

## TEN TEN Digital Gallery

From August 11–16, we're hosting a TEN TEN Digital Gallery, showing a selection of award-winning artwork. Submissions closed August 10, with winners announced on August 16.

## Staying Cool

Beat the heat with yuzu slushies and matcha cocktails, available during happy hour from 3–6 pm. We're also handing out free fans if you need a little extra relief from the summer heat.

Tag us on Instagram when you stop by — we love seeing you enjoy the food, the drinks, and the AC.
