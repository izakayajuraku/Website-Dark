We take something as simple as fries and load them up with Japanese flavors, unusual toppings, and a touch of fun.

Our fries go through a salt brining and triple washing process, followed by double frying, to get them fluffy on the inside and crispy on the outside.

## Our Signature Fries at Izakaya Juraku

### Okonomiyaki Fries

Inspired by Osaka's famous okonomiyaki (savory Japanese pancake), these fries come loaded with:

- Okonomiyaki sauce, a sweet and savory umami-packed glaze
- Shredded cabbage for crunch and freshness
- Cheese and corn for a rich, melty combination
- Scallions and mayo for a creamy, zesty balance
- Katsuobushi (bonito flakes), the final touch, dancing with the heat of the fries

### Loaded Fries

If you're looking for something hearty, our Loaded Fries come in two varieties:

- Kakuni (Japanese braised pork) Loaded Fries: our melt-in-your-mouth kakuni, slow-braised to perfection, served over fries with spicy mayo, melted cheese, and pickled jalapeños
- Karaage Fried Chicken Loaded Fries: our signature karaage (Japanese fried chicken) on top of fries with the same spicy mayo, melted cheese, and pickled jalapeños

### Shake Shake Fries: A Viral Sensation

Our Shake Shake Fries are an interactive, DIY experience that's been making waves online. Guests get a bag with manga-style instructions on how to shake up the perfect fries, plus a side of Q.P. mayo for dipping. There are two flavor options:

- Wasabi Shake Shake Fries: a viral hit, this mix of Kinjirushi wasabi, soy sauce powder, and chicken stock powder brings a bold, umami-rich kick
- Aka Shiso Shake Shake Fries: a unique and refreshing option with red perilla (aka shiso) seasoning, offering a slightly tart and herbal flavor

Guests add the fries and seasoning into the bag, shake it up, and enjoy, just like in Japan.

## How to Enjoy Our Fries at Izakaya Juraku

- Pair them with drinks: fries and a cold beer, highball, or cocktail is always a winning combo
- Share with friends: our fries are perfect for sharing and snacking
- Try all the flavors: each style is a completely different experience
