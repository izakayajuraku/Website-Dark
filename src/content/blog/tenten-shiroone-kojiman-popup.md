On June 30th, we're hosting a special evening celebrating Japanese street art, fashion, beer, and culture at Izakaya Juraku — and it's all for a good cause.

## Who's Involved

- ShiroOne, an internationally recognized Japanese graffiti artist, presenting handmade art and fashion alongside a signing for her new book
- Kojiman, a Tokyo-based artist making his U.S. debut, with work that blends ukiyo-e woodblock print aesthetics with bold modern pop culture references
- FLY HIGH Vintage & Civiatelier NY, back with curated vintage and custom clothing
- An exclusive new beer collab between ShiroOne and The Bronx Brewery, making its Manhattan debut at Juraku

## Details

The event runs from 12:00 PM to 9:00 PM at Izakaya Juraku, 121 Ludlow St, New York, NY 10002. All proceeds benefit TenTen NYC, a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to uplifting artists and fostering community healing through creativity.

Come see the art, meet the artists, and support a great cause.
