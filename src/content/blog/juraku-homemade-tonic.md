At Izakaya Juraku, we make our own house-made Japanese Citrus & Sansho Tonic, a refreshing blend that captures the essence of Japan's unique flavors. It came out of a collaboration with Kankitsu Labo.

## Five Citrus Varieties

The tonic features several Japanese citrus varieties:

- **Yuzu** — aromatic zest and tart juice, providing brightness and floral complexity
- **Amanatsu** — sweetness and depth through its sweet-tart profile and abundant juice
- **Iyokan** — slightly sweeter and more fragrant than yuzu, contributing juiciness and rounded character
- **Kawachi Bankan** — a rare citrus offering mild sweetness and refreshing bitterness
- **Setouchi Lemon** — grown in Japan's Seto Inland Sea region, known for balanced acidity and gentle aroma

## The Sansho Sensation

The blend also incorporates sansho pepper, a Japanese spice known for its lemony aroma and tongue-tingling sensation, which adds a peppery lift to the citrus profile.

## About Kankitsu Labo

Kankitsu Labo focuses on connecting Japan's citrus heritage with American markets while supporting community farmers and introducing authentic Japanese citrus products.
