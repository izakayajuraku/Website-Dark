We wanted to put a more grown-up spin on the classic Japanese chuhai, so we built our Premium Sawa with Nihon no Kokoro Shochu, paired with seasonal Japanese citrus syrups from Umami Insider.

## What Is Sawa?

"Sawa" (サワー) is the Japanese term for a sour-style drink, often referring to what's known in Japan as chuhai. It typically combines shochu or vodka with soda water and fruit flavoring — a mainstay of Japanese izakayas.

## Our Take

Our version leans into premium ingredients and traditional technique. The barley shochu gives it a light but elegant base, while rotating citrus syrups — Amanatsu, Kawachi Bankan, Sudachi — bring their own character, from sweet-tart to zesty and bright.

It's low in alcohol, so it's easy to enjoy over a long meal, and it works well with both grilled and fried dishes. Sophisticated but approachable — good for cocktail people and casual drinkers alike.
