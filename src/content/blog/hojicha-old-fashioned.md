At Izakaya Juraku, we love reimagining classics through the lens of Japanese flavors. One of our signature drinks returning this fall is the Hojicha Old Fashioned, a bold yet balanced cocktail that feels both familiar and refreshingly new.

## A Nod to Tradition, with a Juraku Twist

The Old Fashioned is one of the oldest cocktails in history: simple, spirit forward, and elegant. Last year, we introduced our first version featuring Sato Shiki Single Grain Whisky and a gentle infusion of roasted green tea, or hojicha. The earthy, nutty aroma of hojicha gave the drink a comforting Japanese touch.

This year, we pushed it further.

## The 2025 Edition: Subtle Smoke Meets Roasted Tea

The new Hojicha Old Fashioned layers complexity with just a mist of Laphroaig 10 Year Scotch. Known for its intense peaty smoke, Laphroaig can overwhelm on its own, but here it is used sparingly to introduce a whisper of herbal smokiness that complements the toasted character of hojicha without overpowering it.

The result is a cocktail that is warming, aromatic, and perfect for crisp fall evenings.

## Flavor Notes

- **Base:** Sato Shiki Single Grain Whisky — mellow, rounded, and versatile
- **Accent:** Hojicha — roasted green tea bringing nutty, earthy warmth
- **Finish:** Laphroaig 10yr mist — a touch of smoke and herbs, subtle yet unforgettable

## Experience It at Izakaya Juraku

Find the Hojicha Old Fashioned this fall at Izakaya Juraku. Pair it with our heartier izakaya dishes or simply enjoy it at the bar while watching the city wind down.
