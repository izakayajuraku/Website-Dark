Join us at Izakaya Juraku on February 22nd for an evening built around Nihon Sakari, paired with bites from Umami Insider and Kankitsu Labo.

## About Nihon Sakari Sake

Nihon Sakari has been making sake since 1889. For this event we're pouring a lineup of their sakes, from lighter, easy-drinking styles to more complex bottles worth slowing down for.

## What to Expect

- Guided tastings through a range of Nihon Sakari sakes, each with its own flavor profile
- Bites from Umami Insider chosen to go with the sake
- A little sake education — how it's brewed, how to taste it, and where Nihon Sakari comes from
- Free sake sampling throughout the night
- A few sake cocktails on the menu, made with Nihon Sakari

## Event Details

Date: February 22nd
Location: Izakaya Juraku, Lower East Side, NYC
