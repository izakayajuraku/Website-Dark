Join us at Izakaya Juraku on February 20th for a SATO SHIKI Highball Event — an evening built around Japan's single malt and single grain whisky, poured in a lineup of highballs and cocktails.

## About SATO SHIKI Whisky

SATO SHIKI blends single malt and single grain whisky into something smooth and easy to drink — good neat, good in a highball too.

## The SATO SHIKI Highball Lineup

Highballs are a big part of Japanese drinking culture — whisky and soda, done right, lets the whisky come through instead of getting buried. For this event we put together:

- Classic SATO SHIKI Highball — crisp and balanced
- Yuzu Citrus Highball — bright yuzu on top
- Hojicha Old Fashioned — roasted Japanese tea gives it a deep, aromatic edge
- A few SATO SHIKI cocktails built to show off the whisky's range

## Event Details

Date: February 20th
Location: Izakaya Juraku, Lower East Side, NYC

Come by for whisky, good company, and a chance to try something new if you haven't had Japanese whisky before.
