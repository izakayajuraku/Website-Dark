If you're into Japanese whisky and craft beer, this one's worth paying attention to.

We're pouring Kanosuke Single Malt IPA Cask Finish Whisky, a strange and interesting release from Kanosuke Distillery in Kagoshima, Japan. It's aged in barrels that once held Japanese craft IPA, and the result is an unexpected but genuinely harmonious mix of hoppy citrus bitterness and mellow malt sweetness.

## What Makes It Special

Kanosuke Distillery is known for pushing the boundaries with experimental aging techniques, and this release is a great example. It's finished in barrels previously used for Minami Satsuma IPA, adding floral hop aromas, citrus zest, and bitter spice to Kanosuke's signature soft, round, coastal character.

It's worth ordering if you're an adventurous whisky drinker, especially paired with savory izakaya fare like grilled skewers or our unagi don.

## Available Now at Izakaya Juraku

Come sip this rare bottling at Izakaya Juraku, 121 Ludlow St, New York, NY — neat, with a large ice sphere, or as a luxurious highball. Quantities are limited.
