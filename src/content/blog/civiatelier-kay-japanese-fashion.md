## Rebellion 25: CIVIATELIER Pop-Up

### Fundraiser presented by TEN TEN NYC

CIVIATELIER is a laboratory of reconstruction, where things that were meant to be discarded are given new life. It's not typical recycling or upcycling — the brand describes its work as destruction and creation, transforming unwanted items into desirable pieces. CIVIATELIER has created custom designs for notable figures including KIDINK, HARDHEADED, and Cash Cobain.

The event was presented by TEN TEN NYC, a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit focused on connecting art, artists, and cities through healing. Supporting this fundraiser at Izakaya Juraku contributed to rebuilding CIVIATELIER, advancing sustainable artistry, strengthening local arts communities, and raising mental health awareness.
