Setouchi Japanese Craft Gin is distilled with a lineup of Japanese botanicals built around the Seto Inland Sea, an area known for its citrus groves and coastal climate.

## The Story Behind Setouchi Japanese Craft Gin

Setouchi Gin draws on the Seto Inland Sea's citrus-growing tradition, using locally sourced ingredients that give it a bright, aromatic, citrus-and-herbal profile.

## Setouchi Japanese Craft Gin's Distillation and Botanicals

- Citrus: yuzu, amanatsu, and Setouchi lemons, for a zesty, vibrant character
- Sansho pepper: a touch of Japanese sansho for spice and a light tingle on the palate
- Traditional herbs and classic gin botanicals, rounding out the balance

## How We Use Setouchi Gin at Izakaya Juraku

Setouchi Gin is the base of our House Sansho & Japanese Citrus Gin & Tonic:

- Setouchi Japanese Craft Gin as the citrus-forward base
- Homemade tonic, made in-house from Japanese citrus peels sourced from Kankitsu Labo
- Sansho pepper for spice
- A citrus blend of amanatsu, yuzu, iyokan, kawachi bankan, and Setouchi lemon

It comes out zesty, a little spicy, and smooth.

## How to Enjoy Setouchi Gin at Izakaya Juraku

- Neat or on the rocks, to taste it on its own
- In the House Sansho & Japanese Citrus Gin & Tonic
- In one of our seasonal cocktails — ask your bartender what's pouring

Try it at Izakaya Juraku, 121 Ludlow St, New York, NY 10002. If you like gin with real citrus character, this is worth ordering.
