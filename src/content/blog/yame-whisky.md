We carry a small, carefully chosen lineup of Japanese whisky, and Yame is one of our favorites — elegant, smooth, and made with real craft. We pour both the Yame 10-Year and Yame 15-Year.

## The History of Yame Whisky

Yame Whisky comes from the Kitaya Distillery, a sake and shochu producer with over 200 years of history in Yame, Fukuoka. They brought that same brewing and distilling background into whisky-making, using underground water sourced from the mountains.

## Yame Whisky's Distillation and Aging Process

- A blend of aged malt and grain whisky, chosen for depth and smoothness
- Aged in American oak, mostly ex-bourbon barrels, which brings out vanilla, caramel, and oak
- Aged in Fukuoka's warm, humid climate, which gives it a soft, rounded profile

## Yame Whisky Expressions at Izakaya Juraku

### Yame 10-Year Whisky

- Aged 10 years, smooth and balanced with a rich mouthfeel
- Notes of honey, toasted oak, and dried fruit, with a soft vanilla finish
- The whisky behind our Matcha Highball, paired with Japanese matcha and soda

### Yame 15-Year Whisky

- Aged 15 years, deeper and more complex
- Notes of dark chocolate, roasted nuts, caramel, and a touch of spice
- Long, velvety finish — good neat or on the rocks

## How to Enjoy Yame Whisky at Izakaya Juraku

- Neat or on the rocks, to taste the full range of flavors
- In a Matcha Highball, made with the Yame 10-Year, matcha, and soda
- Alongside grilled meats, yakitori, or seafood

Try Yame at Izakaya Juraku, 121 Ludlow St, New York, NY 10002 — the 10-Year in a Matcha Highball, the 15-Year neat if you want to slow down and taste it on its own.
