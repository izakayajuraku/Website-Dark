Unagi kabayaki is one of the cornerstones of Japanese comfort food, and it's a dish we're proud to serve. Freshwater eel is butterflied, skewered, and grilled, then glazed with a tare sauce made from soy, mirin, sugar, and sake, before a final grilling gives it that glossy finish and deep umami flavor.

Unagi has long held a special place in Japanese culture — traditionally eaten on Doyo no Ushi no Hi (the Midsummer Day of the Ox) for stamina.

## How We Serve It

Our Una Don features grilled unagi kabayaki over steamed white rice, with extra tare sauce and sansho pepper on the side — warm, satisfying, and deeply nostalgic.

Our Unagi Handroll takes it in a more casual direction: a crispy nori cone filled with rice and unagi, easy to eat and great with a cold drink.

Unagi kabayaki is a true izakaya classic — rich and sweet with a hint of smokiness, and a natural match for sake or shochu.
