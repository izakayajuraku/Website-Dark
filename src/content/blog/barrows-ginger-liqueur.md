At Izakaya Juraku, we like building cocktails around distinctive, well-made spirits, and Barrow's Intense Ginger Liqueur is one of our favorites. This Brooklyn-based liqueur is a key ingredient in our signature 212 cocktail, alongside yuzu, shiso, and Japanese gin.

## What Is Barrow's Intense Ginger Liqueur?

It's an all-natural, small-batch product crafted in Brooklyn using fresh, whole ginger with no extracts or artificial flavors. It delivers bold, spicy notes with sweetness and a smooth finish.

## Why We Use It

- Made with 100% fresh ginger root for vibrant, authentic taste
- Contains no artificial ingredients, extracts, or preservatives
- Versatile across citrus-forward, gin, whisky, and rum-based cocktails
- Brooklyn-made, which fits our approach to quality craft ingredients

## The 212 Cocktail

- Japanese Gin — a refined base with herbal and citrus notes
- Yuzu — bright, tart citrus for freshness
- Shiso — a fragrant herb with minty, basil-like character
- Barrow's Intense Ginger Liqueur — bold, warming spice

Together, it's a zesty, herbaceous, and spicy cocktail with real layers of complexity.

Come try the 212 at Izakaya Juraku, 121 Ludlow St, New York, NY 10002.
