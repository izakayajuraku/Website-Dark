Mazemen is brothless ramen — instead of soup, all the flavor lives in the sauce that coats the noodles. Our version comes from a personal place for Chef Kiyo.

During his apprenticeship in Tokyo, Kiyo used to treat himself to mentaiko pasta whenever he could — which, on an apprentice's budget, wasn't often. That memory became the starting point for our Mentaiko Mazemen: ramen noodles tossed with butter, spicy mentaiko (cod roe), cream, and soy sauce, topped with shredded nori and fresh shiso.

It's comfort food with a story behind it, and we think that's exactly what makes it worth ordering.

Come try it at Izakaya Juraku, 121 Ludlow St, New York, NY 10002.
