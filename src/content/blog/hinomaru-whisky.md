One of the more exciting new names in Japanese whisky is Hinomaru, produced by the Kiuchi Distillery — a brewery and distillery with deep roots in Japan.

## The History of Hinomaru Whisky

Hinomaru Whisky is crafted by the Kiuchi Distillery, a family-run business that has been producing sake and shochu for over 200 years. Located in Ibaraki Prefecture, Kiuchi is best known for its Hitachino Nest Beer, one of Japan's most famous craft beer brands. Drawing on their extensive brewing expertise, Kiuchi entered the world of whisky-making, combining traditional Japanese techniques with the influence of Scottish whisky craftsmanship.

## Hinomaru Whisky's Distillation and Aging Process

- Local ingredients and pure water: carefully selected grains and pristine spring water from Ibaraki's mountains
- Copper pot stills: traditional Scottish-style pot stills allow for a rich and complex distillation
- Cask selection: aged in ex-bourbon barrels, sherry casks, and Japanese Mizunara oak, adding layers of vanilla, spice, and umami

## Hinomaru Whisky Expressions at Izakaya Juraku

### Hinomaru Malt Whisky

- A balanced whisky with honeyed sweetness, vanilla, and light citrus notes
- Aged in a combination of ex-bourbon and sherry casks, for a smooth, rounded finish
- Hints of toasted oak and subtle spice, perfect neat or on the rocks

### Hinomaru Mizunara Cask Whisky

- A unique expression aged in Japanese Mizunara oak, imparting complex notes of sandalwood, coconut, and spice
- Silky texture with a long, warming finish
- A rare whisky, and worth trying if you want to see what Mizunara oak does to a spirit

## How to Enjoy Hinomaru Whisky at Izakaya Juraku

- Neat or on the rocks: savor the refined complexity of Hinomaru's aging process
- Whisky highball: a refreshing way to highlight its balanced flavors with soda and ice
- Paired with Japanese cuisine: the depth of Hinomaru pairs beautifully with grilled meats, yakitori, and rich umami dishes

Try Hinomaru Whisky at Izakaya Juraku (121 Ludlow St, New York, NY 10002).
