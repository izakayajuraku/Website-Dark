One of the more unusual beers on our menu is Baladin Fumé, a smoked beer from Italy's Baladin Brewery.

## The Story Behind Baladin Fumé

Baladin Brewery was founded by Teo Musso in 1996 and has been one of the driving forces in Italy's craft beer scene ever since. Fumé is their smoked beer, made with traditional smoking methods and high-quality ingredients.

Fumé is aged in wooden barrels that previously housed whisky, allowing the beer to take on subtle hints of oak, peat, and caramelized malt. That aging process creates a deeply layered beer that balances smoke, sweetness, and earthiness.

## Baladin Fumé's Unique Flavor Profile

- Style: smoked barrel-aged ale
- Aroma: rich notes of charred oak, caramel, and dark fruit
- Flavor: a balance of smoky malt, whisky warmth, and a touch of sweetness
- Mouthfeel: full-bodied and velvety, with a long, lingering smoky finish
- Pairing: grilled meats, umami-rich dishes, and aged cheeses

## Why We Serve Baladin Fumé at Izakaya Juraku

- It brings a unique, smoky depth that pairs exceptionally well with our grilled dishes and yakitori
- It offers a rare, barrel-aged drinking experience that whisky lovers will appreciate
- It's a good showcase of Italian craft brewing done well outside the usual pale ale playbook

Try Baladin Fumé at Izakaya Juraku (121 Ludlow St, New York, NY 10002). If you like your beer with some smoke and depth to it, this one's worth ordering.
