Xyauyù Kioke is an Italian craft beer from Baladin, aged in large Japanese cedar barrels known as kioke.

Instead of keeping it bright and fresh, it's intentionally aged and exposed to slow oxidation: no carbonation, aged in cedar barrels, built to develop over time. This isn't about refreshment. It's about depth.

## Why It's Different

Most beers are designed to be crisp and easy. This goes the other direction — slightly oxidative like fortified wine, fuller body with natural sweetness, meant for slow sipping. Think port or sherry, not IPA or lager.

## Flavor Profile

**Aroma:** Dried fig, raisin, caramel, toasted wood

**Taste:** Rich malt, dark fruit, brown sugar, subtle cedar

**Finish:** Long, warm, slightly oxidative

It leans into that dessert wine territory without losing structure.

## How to Drink It

Take your time with it — lightly chilled or cellar temp, small pours, let it sit and open in the glass. This is something you sip, not rush.
