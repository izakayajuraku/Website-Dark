One of the more interesting names in the new wave of Japanese craft distilleries is Kanosuke Whisky. It comes from Kagoshima, and it's quickly earned a reputation for rich, complex, well-balanced expressions.

## The Story Behind Kanosuke Whisky

Founded in 2017 by Komasa Jyozo, a company with over 130 years of expertise in shochu-making, Kanosuke Distillery sits along the picturesque coastline of Kagoshima Prefecture. Unlike traditional whisky houses, Kanosuke brings a shochu-influenced approach to whisky-making, using barrels previously used for aging their signature Mellowed Kozuru Shochu, adding a unique depth of flavor.

## Kanosuke Whisky's Distillation and Aging Process

Kanosuke Whisky is crafted with a philosophy of "mellowing with time," a concept drawn from the slow, delicate aging process of premium shochu.

- Three pot stills: Kanosuke uses three pot stills of different shapes and sizes, allowing for greater diversity in flavor profiles
- Coastal aging: the barrels are aged in a warehouse facing the sea, where the climate contributes to the whisky's rich character and gentle salinity
- Barrel selection: a mix of American oak, ex-shochu barrels, and even ex-bourbon casks, resulting in a layered, balanced complexity

## Kanosuke Whisky Expressions

- Kanosuke Single Malt 2021: aged in ex-shochu and bourbon casks, with notes of vanilla, caramel, ripe orchard fruits, gentle smoke, and a smooth, mellow finish with hints of salted caramel
- Kanosuke 2022 Limited Edition: a limited-release blend of multiple cask types, with a silky mouthfeel, flavors of honey, baking spices, and roasted nuts, and a long, warming finish with a whisper of sea breeze
- Kanosuke Distillery Exclusive: special releases available only at the distillery, showcasing unique barrel experiments with rich umami depth, often influenced by shochu barrel aging

## How to Enjoy Kanosuke Whisky at Izakaya Juraku

- Neat or on the rocks: experience the delicate balance of flavors in its purest form
- Highball (mizuwari): enjoy it the Japanese way with a refreshing whisky highball
- Paired with Japanese cuisine: Kanosuke's smooth, slightly coastal profile makes it a fantastic match for yakitori, grilled fish, and umami-rich dishes
