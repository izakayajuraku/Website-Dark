Echigo Beer holds a special place on our menu.

As Japan's first craft brewery, Echigo has been setting the standard for Japanese craft beer since 1994. It's known for its dedication to quality ingredients, traditional brewing methods, and smooth, well-balanced flavors.

We proudly serve Echigo Koshihikari, Flying IPA, Red Ale, and Weizen, each offering a unique taste of Japanese brewing craftsmanship.

## The History of Echigo Beer

Echigo Beer was established in Niigata Prefecture, a region known for its pristine water and world-famous rice. It was Japan's first microbrewery, emerging after legal changes in the 1990s allowed smaller breweries to enter the market.

Echigo quickly gained recognition for its commitment to using local ingredients and traditional brewing techniques, making it a pioneer in Japan's growing craft beer scene.

## Echigo Beers Available at Izakaya Juraku

We offer four exceptional Echigo brews, each with its own distinct flavor profile.

### Echigo Koshihikari Lager

Kiyo calls this "the world's best most boring beer," or "what Budweiser would taste like if Budweiser made good beer." While it's a simple beer, Echigo truly nailed the art of a refreshing, easy-drinking lager perfect for any occasion.

- A premium Japanese rice lager brewed with Niigata-grown Koshihikari rice
- Crisp, clean, and smooth, with a subtle sweetness from the rice
- Light body with a refreshing finish, great with sushi, grilled skewers, and fried dishes

### Echigo Flying IPA

- A Japanese take on the classic India Pale Ale
- Aromas of citrus, pine, and tropical fruit
- Balanced bitterness with a smooth, malty backbone, approachable yet bold
- Great with spicy and savory dishes

### Echigo Red Ale

- A rich and malty ale with caramelized sweetness and a slight hop bitterness
- Full-bodied with notes of toasted malt, dried fruit, and roasted nuts
- Ideal for those who enjoy deeper, more complex beer flavors

### Echigo Weizen

- A smooth and refreshing wheat beer brewed in the traditional German style
- Notes of banana, clove, and light citrus, with a creamy mouthfeel
- Light and easy to drink, perfect for a relaxing time
