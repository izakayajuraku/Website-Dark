Ohoro Gin is part of Japan's growing craft gin scene, distilled in Hokkaido using local botanicals that actually taste like the place they're from.

## The Story Behind Ohoro Gin

Ohoro Gin comes from Benizakura Distillery, Hokkaido's first dedicated gin distillery, founded in 2018. They build small-batch gins around local Hokkaido botanicals. The name "Ohoro" comes from the Ainu word meaning "to continue onward."

## Ohoro Gin's Distillation and Unique Botanicals

Ohoro Gin is built on Hokkaido-sourced ingredients:

- Haskap berries, a tart-sweet berry native to Hokkaido, for fruity depth
- Ezo spruce, a coniferous tree that gives it a forest-like aroma
- Japanese peppermint, for a cool, refreshing edge
- Coriander, angelica root, and other classic gin botanicals for balance
- Soft, mineral-rich Hokkaido water for a clean finish

## How We Use Ohoro Gin at Izakaya Juraku

We built our Ohoro Gin & Tonic around it:

- Ohoro Gin as the botanical base
- Japanese citrus and Hokkaido herbs, playing off the gin's forest and fruit notes
- Craft tonic water that doesn't drown out the gin
- A sprig of fresh herbs as garnish

It's light, refreshing, and tastes distinctly like Hokkaido.
