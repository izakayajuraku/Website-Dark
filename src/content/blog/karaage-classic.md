At Izakaya Juraku, karaage ranks among the most popular menu selections. Recognized for its crisp exterior and flavorful seasoning, the dish earned recognition from the New York Times Cooking Channel and remains a guest favorite.

Two variations are available:

- **Signature Karaage** — classic Japanese-style fried chicken served with spicy mayo
- **Oroshi Karaage** — topped with grated daikon and yuzu wasabi for a lighter, more refreshing flavor

The karaage draws inspiration from traditional Japanese izakaya culture, where dishes complement alcoholic beverages and social dining. The preparation method combines Chinese frying techniques with Japanese seasonings including soy sauce, ginger, and garlic, with roots in early 20th-century culinary practices.

Izakaya Juraku maintains these traditional methods while adapting the dish for New York City's dining preferences. The karaage works just as well as an accompaniment to beer or a highball as it does on its own as a snack.
