Shochu represents one of Japan's underappreciated spirits — a remarkably adaptable beverage that Japanese communities have treasured for generations. Though sake dominates international markets, shochu remains the preferred daily drink for numerous Japanese drinkers. Among shochu varieties, imo shochu (sweet potato shochu) distinguishes itself through its rich, earthy depth and complex aroma. At Izakaya Juraku, we highlight this custom by featuring Kurokirishima, an acclaimed imo shochu that exemplifies Kyushu's distillation expertise.

## A Taste of Kyushu: The Legacy of Imo Shochu

Kyushu, the southern island birthplace of imo shochu, benefits from a climate and volcanic terrain that support superior sweet potato cultivation. Japanese shochu production emerged in the 16th century, with imo shochu flourishing in Satsuma (present-day Kagoshima) thanks to plentiful local sweet potatoes. Distillation is what distinguishes shochu from sake, yielding a higher alcohol concentration and more intense flavor.

Kirishima Shuzo manufactures Kurokirishima, recognized as one of Japan's premier imo shochu. Celebrated for its deep umami, velvety texture, and slightly roasted sweetness, it delivers a harmonious yet impactful drinking experience. Black koji mold fermentation, referenced in the "Kuro" part of its name, intensifies its character.

## How We Serve Kurokirishima

We demonstrate imo shochu's adaptability through multiple presentation styles:

- **On the Rocks (Rokku):** ice service reveals its silky qualities and deep sweet potato character, with gradual dilution softening the alcohol while amplifying its natural sweetness
- **Mizuwari (With Water):** this traditional Japanese preparation, blending Kurokirishima with chilled water, emphasizes its delicate floral and earthy undertones
- **Signature Cocktails:** our bar team experiments beyond conventional shochu drinking. The standout Umegroni combines Kurokirishima imo shochu, shiso-infused umeshu, and amaro, delivering a bittersweet, herbaceous balance

## Why You Should Try Imo Shochu

Enthusiasts of whisky, mezcal, or sake should explore imo shochu. It merges characteristics across these spirits with deep complexity, smooth drinkability, and a food-friendly nature. Dishes like grilled skewers, miso preparations, and umami seafood pair beautifully with Kurokirishima, complementing the food without dominating it.

Visiting Izakaya Juraku is a great opportunity to discover imo shochu's sophisticated range. Whether consumed neat, cocktail-style, or by traditional methods, Kurokirishima leaves a memorable impression.
